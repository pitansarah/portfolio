# Notes on FBI BTA

Description: Summary of Chapter 3 of the FBI BAU's *Making Prevention a Reality* entitled "Assessment: Analysis for Guiding Management" transposed onto the 6 step pathway to targeted violence (Grievance, Ideation, Research/Planning, Preparation, Breach, Attack).
[Source: FBI Vaults] (https://vault.fbi.gov/fort-hood-shooting-on-november-5-2009/fort-hood-shooting-on-november-5-2009-final/view)

1. Header / Metadata
1. BLUF
1. Methodology & Info Sources
1. Limitations & Bias
1. Factor Analysis: 
    - 6-Step Pathway to Violence
        > Grievance
        > Ideation
        > Research & Planning
        > Preparation
        > Breach
        > Attack
    - 4- Step Pillar Checklist
1. The Nature of The Threat 
    - Intent: Motivation | *The duration and intensity of violent ideation, the degree to which the person views violence as the solution.*
    - Capability: Progression | *How far subject has moved along the pathway, according to documented evidence indicating the planning, preparation, acquisition of skills/tools, and the sophistication/concreteness of the plan.*
    - Opportunity : Feasibility & Timing | *Level of access subject has to victim, ability to breach setting and exploit vulnerabilities, presence of current stressors or triggers, and any other documented evidence that the attack could occur soon.*
1. Mitigation & Management Plan

## BLUF 

### I. SUMMARY: Checklist Assessment & Weighting 
Assess subject according to the following checklist and indicate weighting of each category. *Adapted from page 21: "Data Interpretation and weighting.*

- Subject's behaviors and personality traits (Weight Rating / Reason)
- A marked trend in the subject's professional/personal interests, relationship patterns, visited places, or recurring conflict (Weight Rating / Reason)
- The social dynamics leading up to the incident including anything that might increase/decrease the likelihood targeted violence (Weight Rating / Reason)
-  The physical setting in which the subejct operates and how this setting increases/ decreases the likelihood of target violence (Weight Rating / Reason)

### II. BLUF (3 sentences total)
- What is the core finding? 
- Why does this matter?
- What do we do now?
