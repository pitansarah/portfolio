1. Factor Analysis: 
    - 6-Step Pathway to Violence
        > Grievance: *Why the subject experiences conflict.*
            - Seeks Revenge   
            - Seeks justice   
            - Seeks fame   
            - Seeks to solve a problem   
            - Desires to kill or be killed   

        > Ideation *Why subject believes that violence is the answer.*    

        > Research & Planning  
            - Present and analyze evidence that shows thinking about violent methods, the target, past similar offenders. Also look for evidence that subject has established symbolic or practical reasons for selecting targets. Identify any overlap of this phase with other phases and make an inference on subject's operational discipline.

        > Preparation   
            - Present and analyze evidence of subject giving away/selling personal belongings, obtaining weapons, practicing using weapons, virtual rehearsals of attack, end of life planning, creation of artifacts with purpose of identifying subject/explaining motive. 

        > Breach   
        > Attack 
        > Pathway velocity  
    - 4- Step Pillar Checklist   
        1. Subject's behaviors and personality traits (Weight Rating / Reason)     
            - *Does the subject exhibit functional/predatory mental illness?* If so: what are key psychiatric symptoms, along with their corresponding behaviors? How does mental illness affect the subject's research/planning and preparation?
            - *Does the subject exhibit a functional personality disorder?* If so: what are key pathological traits, along with their corresponding behaviors? How does the personality disorder affect the subject's research/planning and preparation?   
            - *Evaluate the following psychological risk factors: history of violence, childhood exposure to violence, substance use.*  

        2. A marked trend in the subject's professional/personal interests, relationship patterns, visited places, or recurring conflict (Weight Rating / Reason)   
            - *Evaluate the following behavioral risk factors: ability to organize goal-directed behavior, access to weapons/firearms, history of menacing behavior (i.e.: stalking, harassment), history of legal non-compliance.*

        3. The social dynamics leading up to the incident including anything that might increase/decrease the likelihood targeted violence (Weight Rating / Reason)
            - *Evaluate the following behavioral risk factors: negative family/friend/coworker dynamics, social isolation, instability (e.g.: financial, residential, professional, social).*

        4. The physical setting in which the subject  operates and how this setting increases/ decreases the likelihood of target violence (Weight Rating / Reason)   
1. The Nature of The Threat 
    - **Intent**: Motivation | *The duration and intensity of violent ideation, the degree to which the person views violence as the solution.*
    - **Capability**: Progression | *How far subject has moved along the pathway, according to documented evidence indicating the planning, preparation, acquisition of skills/tools, and the sophistication/concreteness of the plan.*
    - **Opportunity**: Feasibility & Timing | *Level of access subject has to victim, ability to breach setting and exploit vulnerabilities, presence of current stressors or triggers, and any other documented evidence that the attack could occur soon.*
