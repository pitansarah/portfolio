# Overview
*Adapted from “Steps to Analyze Behavior Profiling." [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/981277377/Steps-to-Analyze-Behavior-Profiling)*
1. Profiling Inputs
Review crime-related information. Includes autopsy reports, photographs of the crime scene, and anything that is likely to indicate what happened, how it happened, and why it happened. 
1. Decision Processing 
Attempt to classify crime within FBI manual of behavioral classifications (e.g.: "organized, power assertive serial rapist"). During this stage, also keep note of murder-type (e.g.: mass, spree, serial) classifications. [Douglas: Crime Classification Manual](https://murders.ru/Classific.pdf)
1. Crime Assessment
Reconstruct the crime from the perspective of both the victim and the offender as a chain of events before, during, and after the crime.
1. Criminal Profile
Write a preliminary description including details on the subject's sex, age, race, occupational skills, IQ, social interests, mental health status, and family background. Then state hypothesis on the type of person who committed the crime.
