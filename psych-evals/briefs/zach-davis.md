# Brief: Zachary Davis

## Description
This brief reviews the case of Zachary Davis and establishes a diagnosis framework. Davis was 15 when he killed his mother, Melanie Davis, in Hendersonville, Tennessee, in August 2012, then set fire to the family home while his older brother was inside. He was convicted of first-degree murder, attempted first-degree murder, and aggravated arson and sentenced to life in prison plus 20 years.
