# Low IQ Makes Delusions More Fixed, Exacerbating Schizophrenia

# Description: 
This brief analyzes the commorbidity of schizophrenia and intellectual disability. It distinguishes intellectual disability from  neurological disorders (e.g.: brain disease). It further examines how varying degrees of intellectual impairment alter cognitive flexibility, executive functioning, and real-world problem-solving. Together, these frameworks illustrate how baseline cognitive capacity shapes both the structure of delusions and daily adaptive behavior.
