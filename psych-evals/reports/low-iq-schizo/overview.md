# Overview: Low IQ is a Key Premorbid Risk Factor for Schizophrenia

### Description: 
This brief discusses the commorbidity of schizophrenia and intellectual disability. It analyzes intellectual disability in schizophrenia in distinction to intellectual disability caused by Behavioral Variant Frontotemporal Dementia (bvFTD), a neurological disorder with a similar bioloical etiology. Data is generated on the impact of low IQ on executive function using game theory. In modified game theory paradigms, the participant makes an unfair, aggressive, or mistaken proposal that causes the other party to reject or penalize them (a self-caused social conflict). The executive function being evaluated is how quickly and flexibly the participant adjusts their strategy to repair the interaction (inhibitory control, cognitive flexibility, set-shifting). If the participant cannot assign a positive or negative value this interaction repair without self-causing personal conflict, varying methods of self-defense against self are examined and rated for their self-preservation efficacy.With this data, conclusions are made on the general features of intellectual disability specific to schizophrenic individuals, outside of the scope of the traditional definition of negative symptoms of schizophrenia. 

*This report is coursework for the Bayesian Statistical Modeling course at Brandeis, taught by Dr. Xiaodong Liu (Professor of Psychology, Brandeis).*

Identify a quantifiable cognitive endophenotype of schizophrenia.
