### Objective
Solve the problem that arises from the following thought experiment:
*
Imagine a world in which American adversaries use data on the social, psychological, and behavioral patterns of target individuals, most importantly law enforcement, service members, and government officials. Assume the adversaries exploit this data to psychologically manipulate Americans through offensive attacks crafted to instill mass-terror on a nationwide scale. The primary danger of this weapon would be the inability to address the offense materially, when common methods of addressing issues of a psychological nature (e.g.: religion, social connection, therapeutic intervention) inefficiently address the issue.

In solution, a discreet personal affairs OPSEC service may be offered by individuals who maintain an undetectable, yet legitimate level of proximity to these target individuals. This service would generate an personal OPSEC risk profile, with the goal of defending targets against this imaginary threat. For these servicing analysts, defense would entail suggesting subtle changes to the target individuals order of personal affairs (e.g.: wardrobe, grooming, communication, and daily routines). Offense entails strategic psychological posturing and though-projection, with the aim of disguising said target's identity and introducing noise to the signal, hence making the collection of adversary intelligence difficult/less accurate.
